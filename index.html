Questions

What is VectorProb?

A platform with three parts that share one account balance: prediction markets, trading on forex, commodities and crypto, and copy trading.

How do prediction markets pay out?

Each market has a YES side and a NO side, and the price of a side is its current probability. If your side is right when the market resolves, you receive your stake divided by the probability when you bet, minus a 2% fee on that payout. A $10 bet on YES at 40% returns $24.50. If your side is wrong, the stake is lost.

What do I need before I can bet or trade?

An account, a verified identity for deposits and withdrawals, and at least $10 of available balance. The same $10 minimum applies to placing bets, opening trades and starting to copy someone.

How do leverage and margin work?

Leverage is capped at 1:50 on forex, 1:20 on gold, 1:10 on other commodities and 1:5 on crypto. The margin a trade needs is its position value divided by the leverage, and it has to fit in your available balance, which limits how large a lot size you can use.

How does copy trading work?

Follow an expert predictor or a forex and commodity trader, and their bets or trades are placed on your account automatically. For copy trading, trade sizes are based on your balance when you start following, so winnings do not snowball into larger trades. You can stop following whenever you like.

How do deposits and withdrawals work?

Deposit from your Portfolio page: choose a coin, send funds to your address and submit the transaction. Your balance is credited after confirmation, usually within 30 minutes. For a bank deposit, ask support for the details. Withdrawals are requested from the Portfolio page and need a verified identity.
